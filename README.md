# Measuring Transformation Change (3 hours)

Organizations rolling out Generative AI assistants and coding agents such as Copilot, Claude, and Gemini often mistake license provisioning and initial attendance for real transformation. Without clear success criteria and behavioral metrics, leaders cannot tell whether new tools and training programs are improving daily work or stalling after launch.

This course equips leaders with practical frameworks to define transformation success, measure organizational change management, and design high-impact enablement. Through Do-Nows, guided discussions, and collaborative group activities, participants build change scorecards, role-based training blueprints, and evaluation plans that connect learning to business outcomes.
