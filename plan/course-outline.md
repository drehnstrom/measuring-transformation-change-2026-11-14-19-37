# Course Title: Measuring Transformation Change (3 hours)

## Course Description

Organizations rolling out Generative AI assistants and coding agents such as Copilot, Claude, and Gemini often mistake license provisioning and initial attendance for real transformation. Without clear success criteria and behavioral metrics, leaders cannot tell whether new tools and training programs are improving daily work or stalling after launch.

This course equips leaders with practical frameworks to define transformation success, measure organizational change management, and design high-impact enablement. Through Do-Nows, guided discussions, and collaborative group activities, participants build change scorecards, role-based training blueprints, and evaluation plans that connect learning to business outcomes.

## Learning Objectives

- Measure the effectiveness of organizational change and training across technology transformations
- Define outcome-driven success criteria for Generative AI and coding agent rollouts
- Measure change management progress using balanced leading and lagging indicators
- Design role-based training programs that drive sustained behavioral adoption
- Evaluate training effectiveness and link enablement to measurable business outcomes

## Who Should Attend

Managers, Learning and Development staff, Human Resources professionals, and leaders who want to define and measure the effectiveness of organizational change and training initiatives.

## Prerequisites

- Experience in project management and leadership
